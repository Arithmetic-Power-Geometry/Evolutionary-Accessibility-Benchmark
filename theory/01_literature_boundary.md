# Literature boundary

## Purpose
This file freezes what this project does **not** claim as new and the specific question it will test.

## Established foundations
The project treats the following as established theory, not contributions:
- Wright–Fisher finite-population dynamics.
- Markov representations of evolutionary processes and first-passage/hitting times.
- Weak-mutation / sequential-fixation / origin–fixation reasoning.
- Genotype findability and its relationship to expected hitting time.
- Fitness landscapes, epistasis, historical contingency, mutation-rate evolution, and route multiplicity.

## Mandatory prior work
### McCandlish (2013), *On the findability of genotypes*
For weak mutation on a time-invariant fitness landscape, genotype findability is defined through expected waiting/hitting time to fixation. The work uses a reversible Markov-chain representation and studies determinants of findability. The present project therefore does not claim hitting-time accessibility, Markov evolution, or genotype findability as new.

DOI: 10.1111/evo.12128

### McCandlish & Stoltzfus (2014), *Modeling evolution using the probability of fixation: history and implications*
Origin–fixation / mutation-limited representations are established population-genetic models. The present project treats them as approximation models whose domain of adequacy can be tested, not as a new construction.

DOI: 10.1086/677571

## Boundary of the present project
The project asks a model-comparison question:

> Under what conditions does a specified simplified evolutionary probability model reproduce finite-population target-hitting probabilities within a prespecified tolerance, where does that approximation cease to be adequate, and which departures from its assumptions control the magnitude and direction of error?

The intended contribution is the systematic characterization of model-relative approximation error, tolerance-dependent adequacy regions and boundaries, recovery regimes, and the displacement of those boundaries under controlled assumption perturbations.

## Claim discipline
No file or manuscript generated from this repository should claim that this project invented evolutionary accessibility, genotype findability, hitting times, Markov evolutionary dynamics, Wright–Fisher dynamics, fixation probability, fitness landscapes, epistasis, or origin–fixation theory.

Novelty claims remain provisional until the expanded literature audit and computational results support them.
