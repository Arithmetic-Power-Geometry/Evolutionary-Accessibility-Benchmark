## Interpretation

This is direct empirical evidence that the Cit+ phenotype in this experimental system was reached repeatedly through more than one structural mutational class. It supports the limited Stage-6 statement that alternative routes to a target phenotype can occur in experimental evolution.

It does not establish that all 14 events are probabilistically independent in a model-theoretic sense, does not estimate route probabilities, and does not demonstrate that route multiplicity by itself causes an approximation-adequacy boundary crossing.

## Stage-6 synthesis

Together with E1/E2 from Dryad 10.5061/dryad.6226d, the empirical stage now establishes three approximation-relevant observations:
1. strong mutation-burden/state heterogeneity among LTEE populations;
2. strong mutation-spectrum concentration differences between mutator and nonmutator populations;
3. repeated Cit+ realization through at least two structural mutational route classes.

These observations motivate the biological relevance of the controlled stressors without being used as causal validation of the approximation-discrepancy framework.
